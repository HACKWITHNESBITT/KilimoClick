About KilimoClick

KilimoClick was built to bridge the gap between complex satellite data and the daily realities of smallholder farmers in the Lake Victoria Basin. By translating high-resolution ESA WorldCover, SoilGrids, and DEM rasters into simple, actionable insights, we empower communities to plant the right crops and manage water efficiently.

Mission

Enhance food security and climate resilience through data-driven agricultural planning.

For the Community

Designed specifically for the Kisumu region, accounting for local soil profiles and seasonal weather patterns.
